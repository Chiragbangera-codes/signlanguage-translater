#
# Selection notes
# ---------------
# The 33 below the first heading are the current shipping vocabulary. The 17
# additions were chosen for three things: they compose into sentences, they
# cover gaps in the existing set (no people, no verbs of perception, only two
# emotions), and their handshapes and motions differ from each other.
#
# That last point matters more than it looks. preprocess.py moves each hand's
# wrist to the origin and scales by hand size, which deliberately removes
# camera position — but it also removes WHERE the hand sits relative to the
# body. Two signs with the same handshape at different body locations are
# therefore near-identical to this model. `mom` (thumb at chin) and `dad`
# (thumb at forehead) are exactly that pair; both are included because they are
# high-value words, but check the confusion matrix after training and drop one
# if they trade. The same caution applies to any future grandma/grandpa pair.
